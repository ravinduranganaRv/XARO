Good to know

How long does an order take?

Drop 001 sets dispatch in 10 to 14 business days. Wall art takes 4 to 6 weeks. Custom pieces add design approval time. Carrier time is extra and varies by destination.

Where do you ship?

Are duties and taxes included?

Returns and damage

Custom pieces cannot be returned for change of mind. If anything arrives damaged, defective or wrong, tell us within 14 days of delivery and we will repair or replace it. Standard pieces can be exchanged within 14 days.

How do I care for my rug?

Vacuum gently on low suction, spot clean with a damp cloth, keep away from heat. Never machine wash.
